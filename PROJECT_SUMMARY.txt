THE HEURISTIC (Key Innovation!)
  Formula: h(n) = α × distance_estimate + β × risk_estimate
  
  Where:
  - α = distance weight (0-1)
  - β = safety weight (0-1)
  - Automatically adjusts based on user profile!

RISK MULTIPLIER SYSTEM
  Male             → 1.0x
  Female           → 1.5x (50% more vulnerable)
  
  Traveling Group  → 1.0x
  Traveling Alone  → 1.3x (30% more risky)
  
  Day              → 1.0x
  Evening          → 1.2x
  Night            → 1.8x (80% more risky)
  
  Example: Female + Alone + Night = 1.5 × 1.3 × 1.8 = 3.51x risk!
